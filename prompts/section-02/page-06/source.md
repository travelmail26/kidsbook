Page 6 — The Chalkboard: Time Spent Eating
That evening, Aiden told his parents what happened.

“Trevor eats for such a long time,” he said. “Why doesn’t he come play?”

Dad and Mom pulled out the chalkboard.

This time, he drew clocks.
“Each clock represents one hour spent eating,” Dad said.
He drew two clocks under Aiden.
“Humans spend about two hours a day eating.”
Then he drew six clocks under Trevor.
“Primates can spend around six hours eating; that’s three times as long.”
Aiden looked from the two clocks to the six.
“Oh! So, I have more time to play and explore!”
